Each variant is its own component with its own code: install the one you need. **Default** and **Detailed** put the photos above the card's surface. **Overlay**, **Overlay detailed** and **Booking** cover the whole card with the photos and draw the text over a dark gradient, so it reads the same in light and dark mode.

With several photos, every variant swipes through them with a [Carousel](/docs/atoms/carousel) and shows white dots on the photo.

The spec icons come from your [icon registry](/docs/atoms/icon). Add the ones you use to `icons.tsx`, for example with `expo-symbols`:

```tsx title="components/ui/icons.tsx"

bath: symbol("bathtub", "bathtub"),
area: symbol("square.dashed", "square_foot"),
rooms: symbol("door.left.hand.closed", "door_front"),
```

`price` is a string: format it yourself, currency included, so the card doesn't decide how your app shows money. `images` takes one photo or several; with one, there is no carousel and no dots.

## Props

<VariantPanel group="listing-card" value="default" active>

| Prop          | Type                    | Default | Description                                                                                           |
| ------------- | ----------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `title`       | `string`                | —       | Name of the place, on one line next to the price.                                                     |
| `price`       | `string`                | —       | Already formatted, currency included: `"$3,679"`.                                                     |
| `priceUnit`   | `string`                | —       | Shown small after the price: `"/mo"`, `"/night"`.                                                     |
| `subtitle`    | `string`                | —       | One line under the title: a tagline, an area.                                                         |
| `images`      | `ImageSourcePropType[]` | —       | One photo, or several to swipe through.                                                               |
| `aspectRatio` | `number`                | `4 / 3` | Of the photos.                                                                                        |
| `badge`       | `string`                | —       | Short label in a `highlight` [Badge](/docs/atoms/badge), on the photo.                                |
| `specs`       | `ListingSpec[]`         | —       | `{ icon?, value, label? }`: beds, baths, area. Shown as "**2** Beds", separated by lines. Three fit.   |
| `onPress`     | `() => void`            | —       | Makes the whole card pressable. The card is read as "title, price".                                   |
| `style`       | `StyleProp<ViewStyle>`  | —       | Extra styles for the card container, for example a width in a rail.                                   |

</VariantPanel>

## Accessibility

- A pressable card is read as one element: "title, price" (the Detailed variants: "price, title").
- With several photos, the dots are read as "Photo 1 of 4" and the carousel is labelled "Photos".
- The overlay variants draw white text on a dark gradient in both color schemes: the photo stays the same in dark mode, so the text does too. Pick photos without large white areas at the bottom.
