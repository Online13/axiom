---
title: Blocks
description: "Complete or near-complete sections of an app, built from primitives and compositions."
---

A block is a section of an app or a whole screen: a settings group, a sign-in form, a paywall. It is built from [primitives](/docs/components#primitives) and [compositions](/docs/compositions), uses realistic content, and is a starting point to adapt to your product like any other copied file.

```text
Primitives  →  Compositions   →  Blocks
Item        →  Settings Item  →  Settings Section
```

Blocks sit at the concrete end of the [component hierarchy](/docs/components). A primitive is generic, a composition is one concrete interface, a block puts several of them together into a part of an app.
