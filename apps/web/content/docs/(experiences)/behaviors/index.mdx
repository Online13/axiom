Axiom doesn't stop at components. Over time, it covers the behaviors every mobile app needs. A behavior is an interaction mechanic, such as a gesture or a reaction to scroll, usually backed by a hook. Components live in [atoms](/docs/atoms), [molecules](/docs/molecules), [organisms](/docs/organisms) and [templates](/docs/templates); complete flows such as search or loading states live in [patterns](/docs/patterns).

Each card links to the behavior's page and shows its status:

- **Available**: a component or hook you can install today.
- **Spec**: the behavior is described, but not implemented yet.
- **Planned**: on the list, not described yet.
