Components are only the beginning. Patterns describe how they behave together on a real device.

<Callout title="Planned">
  Patterns come after the component layers. Depending on their size, each one may become an organism, a template, a block or its own registry entry.
</Callout>

## Behaviors

The recurring problems Axiom sets out to solve once: scroll, gestures, contextual UI, navigation, app states, search and async content.
