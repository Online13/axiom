<Callout title="Planned">Not built yet. Chapter is the typography and theming test: a whole screen made of text.</Callout>

A library, a reading surface, and the notes that come out of it. Almost no chrome: what is left is type, spacing, color and one contextual toolbar.

<ShowcaseScreens app="chapter" />

## Why this app

A reader exposes the [tokens](/foundations/tokens) with nothing to hide behind:

- Line height, measure and paragraph spacing come from the [type scale](/foundations/tokens/typography), not from magic numbers.
- Reading themes — light, sepia, dark, black — are [color roles](/foundations/colors) swapped underneath, with no component aware of it.
- The app has to stay readable at the largest system font size without any layout breaking.
- Text selection drives a [dynamic toolbar](/behaviors/dynamic-toolbar) instead of a system menu.

## Screens

| Screen | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Library | Covers, progress, filters, grouped by status. |
| Reader | The page, the progress bar, tap zones for chrome. |
| Highlight | A selection and its contextual toolbar. |
| Notes | Highlights and notes, searchable, per book. |

## Built with

- [Title](/atoms/title) and [Text](/atoms/text) as the whole reading surface.
- [Theme](/foundations) with four reading themes, switched at the token layer.
- [Long press](/behaviors/long-press) for selection, [dynamic toolbar](/behaviors/dynamic-toolbar) for its actions.
- [Menu](/atoms/menu), [Slider](/atoms/slider) and [SegmentedControl](/atoms/segmented-control) for reading settings.
- [AppBar](/templates/app-bar) that [hides on scroll](/behaviors/hide-on-scroll) while reading.

## What it checks

- The type scale holds a full page of prose, not just labels and buttons.
- Adding a theme means adding colors, never touching a component.
- Chrome that hides on scroll comes back reliably, including after a tap.
