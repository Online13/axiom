---
title: Hooks
description: "Reusable mobile behaviors as hooks."
---

Hooks hold behavior without any UI. Each one is usable on its own, without adopting the rest of the system.

<Cards>
  <Card title="useRefreshControl" href="/hooks/use-refresh-control">
    Pull-to-refresh for any list or ScrollView.
  </Card>
  <Card title="useHideOnScroll" href="/hooks/use-hide-on-scroll">
    Bars that slide out on scroll down and come back on scroll up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="useCollapsibleHeader" href="/hooks/use-collapsible-header">
    A large header that shrinks into a compact bar as the list scrolls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="useScrollToTop" href="/hooks/use-scroll-to-top">
    A scroll-to-top button past a threshold, plus tab press and status bar tap.
  </Card>
  <Card title="useScrollAwareAppBar" href="/hooks/use-scroll-aware-app-bar">
    An AppBar that gets a background, a hairline and its title as content scrolls under it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="useSwipeActions" href="/hooks/use-swipe-actions">
    Actions revealed by swiping a row, with an optional full swipe to delete.
  </Card>
  <Card title="useDragToReorder" href="/hooks/use-drag-to-reorder">
    Lift a row and drag it to a new position in a list.
  </Card>
</Cards>
