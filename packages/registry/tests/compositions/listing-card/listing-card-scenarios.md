# ListingCard

## Covered by `listing-card.test.tsx`

- Shows the title, the price, the subtitle and the badge.
- Shows each spec, its value with its label.
- Pressable: one button read as its main line and its price.
- Without `onPress` it is no button.

## Checked on a device

- Long titles and prices truncate on small screens; the text stays readable over the photo.
