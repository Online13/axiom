# StatsCardRing

## Covered by `stats-card-ring.test.tsx`

- A `progressbar` read as one sentence with the percentage, valued 0–100.
- Past the goal, it says the real percentage (130%) but the value stays at 100; a negative progress counts as 0.
- Pressable: the whole card is one button with the same sentence, and no separate progressbar.

## Checked on a device

- The bar or ring fills, and turns green past the goal.
